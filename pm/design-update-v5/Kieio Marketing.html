How it works

Three steps.
One life.

You write a single line. KIEIO builds a world from it. NPCs arrive — with memory, with opinions, with their own agendas. The story remembers you back.

  1. 01
    Write a seed
    A line, a paragraph, a premise. "An inn at midnight. The keeper studies a stranger." That's enough.
  2. 02
    Enter the world
    KIEIO renders a place, populates it, and hands you the protagonist. The narrator listens before they speak.
  3. 03
    Live a life
    Speak, act, choose. Every consequence is weighed. NPCs remember. The world bends around what you've done.
Step 01 · Seed
An inn at midnight. The keeper studies a stranger who arrived too late.
客棧。半夜。掌櫃望住一個遲到嘅陌生人。
Step 02 · World
The inn smells of cold tea and damp wool. Mei sets down her ledger. The rain hasn't let up in three days. "You're late," she says. She doesn't ask your name.

Step 03 · Choose
Mei is waiting. The fire crackles. What do you say?

Memory architecture

NPCs remember.

Not the last three messages. Not a shallow cache. KIEIO uses a four-layer memory system that holds what you've done, who you've hurt, who you've loved — for two hundred turns and beyond.

Layer 01 · Episodic

What just happened

The last few exchanges. Tone, mood, the immediate beat. Resolves "Mei looks at you" without asking who Mei is.

turn 247 — recall 100%
Layer 02 · Semantic

Who is who

Names, relationships, the shape of the world. Mei is the innkeeper. The river floods every spring. You are a courier who lost something.

turn 247 — recall 96%
Layer 03 · Emotional

How they feel

Trust, suspicion, affection, grudges. NPCs carry their emotional state forward. They will hold a grudge for two hundred turns.

turn 247 — Mei trust 38/100
