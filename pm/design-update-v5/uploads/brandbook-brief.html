1 · What Kieio is

Kieio is an AI-powered interactive fiction RPG platform built for the Chinese-language internet (Hong Kong, Taiwan, overseas Chinese diaspora · 繁體中文 default).

Users either write their own story seed, play a community-shared story, or pick from official curated stories. AI then generates a custom game world — including a tailor-made interface, persistent characters with their own personalities, and an evolving narrative that remembers everything the player does.

Unlike a chatbot, Kieio is a place to live another life. A turn-based, choice-driven, consequence-heavy world where NPCs have opinions, push back, and remember.

「每個人都可以走入自己嘅故事，做主角。」 — Founder positioning statement

3 · Why it exists

The big international platforms (AI Dungeon, Character.AI, NovelAI, Replika) do not serve the Chinese-language market. Their UI, story library, character archetypes, and AI tuning are all built for English-speaking Western audiences. Chinese users get bad translations, no curated Chinese stories, and AI that doesn't understand 繁中 cultural context (HK slang, TW idioms, web novel tropes).

Beyond market gap, Kieio also fixes three industry-wide design failures that drive churn on every existing platform:

PROBLEM 1

AI forgets

After 50 turns, the AI doesn't remember what happened in turn 5. Player frustration #1.

PROBLEM 2

Yes-man AI

NPCs always agree, always reward the player. No real consequence. No real character.

PROBLEM 3

Generic interface

Every story uses the same chat UI. A romance story and a dungeon crawl look identical. Immersion destroyed.

4 · Core product pillars

Six things make Kieio different. The brandbook should reflect that this product has technical depth — it's not a toy chatbot wrapper.

① Adaptive Interface (故事自適應介面)

AI generates a custom UI for each story. A romance story gets affection meters, NPC mood displays, relationship axes. A wuxia adventure gets HP/internal-energy bars, technique inventories. A modern detective story gets clue boards, suspect dossiers. No two stories look the same.

② MemPalace — 4-layer Persistent Memory

Recent turns held in full · scene-level rolling summaries · semantic vector retrieval (pgvector) · automatic lorebook that catalogs people, places, items, events. NPCs remember what you said 200 turns ago. Story continuity that current platforms can't match.

③ Narrative Integrity Engine

Every story has a Story Bible (immutable world rules · central conflict · 3–5 act milestones). Every character has a Character Card with red lines they will not cross. A Director AI vets every player action — risky moves trigger skill checks with dice rolls and permanent consequences. NPCs push back. The story has weight.

④ NPC Level 3 Generative Agents

Premium tier feature: up to 3 NPCs per turn run their own parallel AI agent. Each NPC has an inner thought stream, intentions, and a chain-of-thought reasoning trace the player can read in the "Memory Journal · NPC Inner Voices" tab. Each NPC is its own perspective, not just a puppet of the narrator.

⑤ Multi-LLM Choice

Users pick their preferred AI model — Claude (Anthropic), GPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google), Grok (xAI), or open-source via OpenRouter. Credits work universally across models. Adult content uses a separately routed pipeline.

⑥ Three entry points, one engine

Write your own story seed · play a community story (fork & play) · pick an official curated story. Same engine powers all three.

5 · User journey

StepWhat happens
Discovery Lands on Library (Netflix-style covers) or Visitor landing (3-pillar hero). Browses official stories, community trending, genre carousels.
Creation Either writes a seed prompt (a few sentences of premise) OR forks an existing community story. AI generates: state schema (custom UI fields) → story bible → 1–6 character cards with red lines → opening narrative. 4-task generation dashboard shows progress.
Play Turn-based. Player types or picks an action. Director AI vets it (allow / reject / skill check / allow-with-constraint). If skill check → dice rolls inline, 4 outcomes (crit success / success / fail / crit fail). Narrator AI streams the response. State updates. NPCs have new dispositions. Memory grows.
Memory Journal Dedicated tab. Shows lorebook entries auto-extracted, scene summaries, NPC inner thoughts (premium tier), the unfolding story arc. Player can see the AI actually remembers.
Continue / Share Sessions persist forever. Player can mark a playthrough public to share with friends or the community. Others can fork it and play their own version.

6 · Brand soul

This section is the most important one for the brandbook. Not what Kieio looks like — what Kieio feels like.

SOUL 01

A place, not a toy

Kieio is somewhere you go. You don't "use" it for 5 minutes. You live in it for an evening. The brand should feel inhabited, lived-in — not gamified or playful in a kid-friendly way.

SOUL 02

Tailored, not generic

Every story is its own world. The brand needs to communicate "made for you" — the platform shapes itself around your premise. Like a tailor, not a vending machine.

SOUL 03

Respect over flattery

NPCs don't tell players they're right when they're wrong. Choices have weight. The brand voice should reflect that — direct, intelligent, treats the user as a peer. Never sycophantic, never patronizing, never "魔法世界等緊你哦~" cute.

SOUL 04

Asia, not ethnic

Kieio is Chinese-language-first but the aesthetic should feel internationally Asian — closer to Studio Ghibli, mubi, A24, 設計家 — than to ethnic-themed mascots or red-lantern stereotype. Sophisticated Asian, not "Chinatown".

SOUL 05

Quiet intelligence

The product does heavy technical work (4-layer memory, parallel agents, adaptive UI generation) but the user shouldn't feel that complexity. The brand should feel calm and confident, not loud or feature-bragging.

SOUL 06

Mystery has value

The name "Kieio" is intentionally abstract — it doesn't tell you what the product does. That's the point. Like Notion, Linear, Arc, Atlas — names that become meaningful through use, not on first read. The brand should embrace that mystique, not over-explain itself.

9 · Functional constraints

These are the non-creative constraints — practical requirements the brand must work within. Everything else is open creative territory.

  • 繁體中文 first — primary content language. Simplified Chinese + English ready Day 1 but secondary.
  • Mobile-first — wordmark and logo must read at small sizes (16px favicon · app icon · social avatar).
  • Pronunciation aid needed — most users will see "Kieio" before they hear it. The brand system should somehow help them say it right ("KEE-yo" or 「讀 KEE-yo」). Founder uses 「讀『KEE-yo』」in product copy already.
  • Adult mode coexistence — brand must be able to gracefully handle the existence of NSFW content without becoming an "adult brand". Think: Reddit, AO3, Discord — not OnlyFans.
  • Accessibility — minimum 4.5:1 contrast for body text (WCAG AA). Type system must support 繁中 (PingFang TC / Noto Sans TC / etc.) at long-read sizes.
  • International future — eventually launches in EN markets too. The brand must not be locked to Chinese aesthetics.
  • Domain — kieio.com is live.

10 · Brandbook deliverables

What we hope a full Kieio brandbook would cover. Not prescribed — explore freely:

Logo system

Wordmark · symbol/mark · monogram · lockups · sizing rules · clear space · what NOT to do.

Color

Primary palette · accent · neutral grays · semantic (success/warning/error) · how to apply across light + dark surface types.

Typography

Display + body + monospace pairings · Chinese + Latin coexistence · size scale · weight system · line-height guidelines.

Voice & tone

How Kieio writes. Examples of on-brand vs off-brand copy. How it greets users, gives errors, explains features.

Iconography

Icon system that works alongside the existing lucide-react foundation, or a custom suggestion.

Photography / illustration

Story cover art direction · NPC avatar treatment · empty-state illustrations · marketing-page imagery.

Sample applications

How the brand shows up: app icon · favicon · social cards · email · landing page hero · merch (if relevant) · in-product surfaces.

Logo ideation exploration

3–5 distinct directions for the logo, each with rationale. We want range — show us we have options before converging.

Important · creative latitude: Don't anchor on what the current Kieio product looks like (the v5 Grok × Netflix UI mockup). That was a functional UI exercise. The brand can — and probably should — push further. Start from soul (Section 6), not from the existing screens.
