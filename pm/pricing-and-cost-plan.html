• Director call: 2K input · 300 output (always Claude Haiku 4.5)

• Moderation: free (OpenAI Moderation API)
• Embedding: 200 tokens at $0.02/1M = $0.000004/turn
• Memory overhead (RAG + summarizer + lorebook · amortized): ~$0.001/turn
